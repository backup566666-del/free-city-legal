Free City Moderator Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Free City Moderator Bot ("the Bot") handles information when it is used on Discord.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled on a server, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord role information
  • Moderation actions and warnings
  • Messages or message-related information required for moderation
  • Bot commands and interactions
  • Server configuration information
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the Bot

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide moderation functionality, detect potential rule violations, maintain moderation logs, process warnings, provide administrative features, protect the Bot from abuse, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve security and reliability.

3. Message Content

Where the Bot has the required Discord permissions or intents, it may process message content for moderation or security purposes. Message content is not intended to be retained unnecessarily.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the Bot, provide requested services, protect security, comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, or investigate abuse and security incidents.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for Bot functionality, moderation records, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord and the Discord API. Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own policies and terms.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to independently collect information from children. Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the information and functionality involved, you may request information about or deletion of data associated with your use of the Bot through the official Free City support channels.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The latest version will remain available at this public URL.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the Free City Administration through the official Free City support channels.
